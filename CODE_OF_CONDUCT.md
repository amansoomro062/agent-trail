# Code of Conduct

Be decent. This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant:

- Be respectful and constructive, in issues, PRs and everywhere else.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Assume good intent; disagree with the work, not the person.
- Maintainers may remove content and ban contributors who don't follow this.
